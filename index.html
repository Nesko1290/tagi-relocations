Why Geneva is different

A market built for people who already live here.

Listings vanish within hours. Agencies (régies) choose between dozens of complete files. Leases are written in French. And newcomers arrive without the Swiss references landlords expect. We close that gap.

  1. 01

    The best flats never reach the portals

    Many apartments are let through agency contacts before they are published. Two decades of relationships mean we hear early.

  2. 02

    Your file has to win, not just qualify

    No Swiss debt record, no local payslips, a carte de légitimation instead of a permit. We assemble a file that régies recognise and trust.

  3. 03

    Every clause, explained in your language

    Rental deposit, notice periods, entry inspection, indexation — we walk you through the lease before you sign, so nothing surprises you later.

  4. 04

    You may not even be here yet

    We visit on your behalf, film live video tours and handle the paperwork, so you can arrive with keys rather than a hotel booking.

Questions

What colleagues usually ask first.

Something else on your mind? Write to us — we reply within one working day.

Can you start before I arrive in Geneva?

Yes, and it's usually the best moment to start. We can search, visit on your behalf with live video and prepare your application, so you can sign soon after arrival.

I don't have a Swiss permit, bank account or debt record. Is that a problem?

Not with the right file. International civil servants usually hold a carte de légitimation rather than a residence permit, and agencies accept an employer attestation in place of local payslips. We know which documents each agency expects and prepare them with you.

We have children. Can you find us something near their school?

Yes — it's one of Maureen's specialities. Many families choose Versoix, Genthod or Bellevue for the Collège du Léman and the other schools on the north shore, or Grand-Saconnex for the Campus des Nations. Tell us the school and we'll search around it.

How long does a search usually take?

It depends on your budget, timing and how flexible you are on location, but most searches conclude within a few weeks. We'll give you a realistic estimate on our first call.

How much deposit will I need?

Swiss law caps the rental deposit at three months' rent, held in a blocked bank account in your name. Rental guarantee insurance is an alternative that avoids tying up cash — we'll explain both.

Can I live in France or the canton of Vaud instead?

Many international staff live in the Pays de Gex or along the lake in Vaud. Rents and space differ, as do schools, taxes and the daily commute. We'll talk through the trade-offs honestly before you decide.

What does your service cost?

Our fee depends on the services you need. After the free discovery call we send a clear written quote — no surprises. Some employers reimburse relocation support; we provide invoices in the format your HR needs.
