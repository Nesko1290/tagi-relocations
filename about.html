About us

From Kenya to Geneva, a life in the international community.

Maureen Tagi came to Switzerland at 25 for her first diplomatic posting. More than forty years later — after a career as a Kenyan diplomat and at the United Nations — she is Swiss, she knows Geneva like the back of her hand, and she helps fellow expats find a home and a school for their children.

Maureen's path

Forty years, one home.

  1. 1985

    A first diplomatic posting

    At 25, Maureen arrives in Switzerland to serve Kenya as a diplomat, first in Zurich. Like every newcomer, she has to find her feet, a home and a way through Swiss paperwork.

  2. Geneva

    The move to International Geneva

    Her career brings her to Geneva, the city she has called home ever since. She serves with the Kenyan diplomatic service until the early 2000s.

  3. 2000s

    The United Nations, at UNHCR

    Maureen joins the UN Refugee Agency, where she works until 2024 — two decades alongside colleagues arriving from every corner of the world.

  4. Always

    The friend everyone calls

    Throughout those years, fellow expats ask her where to live, how to win an apartment and which school to choose. She helps, one family at a time.

  5. Today

    Tagi Relocations

    Retired from the UN, Maureen turns that experience into a family business — home, schools, insurance and banking, from her base in Versoix.

A family business

Maureen leads every search. Her family is beside her.
